# Terrain DEM delivery and verification

Status: no source is enabled for production. Public endpoint availability and open data licences do not grant a hosting capacity or commercial-service commitment.

- [Mapterhorn data access](https://mapterhorn.com/data-access) documents Terrarium RGB WebP 512-pixel XYZ tiles and TileJSON, plus bounded PMTiles extracts. Its [attribution catalogue](https://download.mapterhorn.com/attribution.json) identifies Geoscience Australia 5 m LiDAR DEM in parts of Australia as CC BY 4.0. The [project page](https://mapterhorn.com/) states global 30 m and partial Australia 5 m coverage. Coverage, source resolution, vertical datum and accuracy cannot be assumed for a particular sample without provenance. Published data-access material inspected on 29 October 2026 did not specify multi-user commercial SaaS terms, rate limits, reliability or an SLA. Commercial hosted use remains unverified; do not configure this endpoint in production without confirmation.
- [Geoscience Australia 5 m LiDAR DEM](https://pid.geoscience.gov.au/dataset/ga/89644), as identified in Mapterhorn's catalogue, is CC BY 4.0 and has partial coverage. Data reuse and hosted-service availability are separate. Its vertical datum, source-specific coverage and access/processing requirements must be verified before production use.
- Mapterhorn documents bounded PMTiles extracts. These could form a local proof of concept if each included source's rights, attribution and datum are reviewed. A PMTiles archive cannot simply be passed to the current XYZ TileJSON raster-dem source: it requires an appropriate protocol adapter or a tile server, HTTP byte-range support, a suitable storage location and a tested MapLibre integration. No hosting capacity, production cost or permission has been established, and no service was provisioned.

A bounded live-data check retrieved one 512×512 zoom 11 tile at each of Vermont South, Balliang, Lismore, Dunoon and the Australian Alps; Terrarium decoding returned approximately 110, 192, 10, 151 and 2,221 m respectively. These are unvalidated pixel samples, not survey measurements, proof of vertical datum, parcel alignment, or screenshots of application rendering. Point coordinates were approximate. The live endpoint was used only for the bounded investigation, not enabled in production.

In the isolated fixture browser, a synthetic DEM passed centre sampling, terrain toggle, exaggeration selection, hillshade toggle and profile drawing. A separate existing parcel multi-select test passed. This does not verify real terrain geometry or live VIC/NSW parcel services. Chromium reported `net::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID` for `https://spatial.planning.vic.gov.au/` and `https://mapprod3.environment.nsw.gov.au/` behind the environment proxy; curl's TLS verification succeeded for both (HTTP 200 and 302 respectively). Installing the available environment CA certificates in an isolated Chromium test trust directory did not resolve this. TLS validation was not disabled.

A separate application proof used nine cached real Mapterhorn z9 Terrarium tiles around the workspace's default Ballarat-area centre, served locally to the opt-in test configuration. In Chromium the actual LandOS workspace enabled 3D and returned approximately 446.4 m at its centre. This is evidence of decoding and application sampling of a real DEM tile, not verification of the five requested places, visible relief accuracy, licence, datum, or commercial hosting permission. A synthetic DEM screenshot was captured at 1024×768; it shows the terrain controls, hillshade and a drawn profile. After screenshot capture the subsequent 2D button click timed out in the software renderer; a separate fixture run without the screenshot passed that transition.
