# Parcel multi-selection release

Rollback baseline: `4027b6b798bbf36c78e51e63424ea45d2509e483`. The current V2 design and the single MapLibre renderer remain in place. The supplied MaxQI `/land` source was inspected read-only for Shift selection and summary behaviour; MaxQI was not modified or browser-tested.

The Dock keeps accessible Spatial Tools and Search icons while removing the visible Draw caption and duplicate header search. The shortcut opens and focuses Search. Layer children are listed through a workspace-scoped, paginated read endpoint, and a single complete feature can be fetched without downloading all other features. Layer visibility and device-scoped opacity controls remain separate from child selection. Legacy features use SHA-256-based in-memory identifiers with collision handling; a layer with missing, unsupported or duplicate stored IDs is view-only for editing so unrelated identities cannot be rewritten.

Normal parcel clicks replace the selection; Shift-click or the touch Multi-select mode toggles items. Selected parcels and polygon features have simultaneous outlines, numbered boundary markers, source/type labels, individual removal, Fit to All and Clear All. Full geometry is loaded before calculating geodesic area. The summary flags unavailable boundaries and potentially overlapping geometry; overlap is not silently subtracted and the calculation is not a surveyed title area. Selection is limited to 50 items. Location-only records are not turned into invented boundaries. No selection automatically writes or modifies a Workspace record.

On the local isolated backend, desktop Shift and simulated iPhone touch selection, basemap switching, Fit/Clear, numbered markers, overlapping feature chooser, child-layer selection, pagination, anonymous and foreign-workspace denial passed on the final build. RLS and concurrency tests passed in a disposable database. Existing Field, Spatial, Parcel, Planning, Drawing recovery, Share, MapLibre, workspace, import, photo and change-feed regressions passed; Spatial also passed on the final build. Lint, typecheck, 43 unit tests and the production build passed on the current source. No physical iPhone/iPad or authenticated production account has been tested.

The unchanged ten-frame performance assertion on the preceding candidate failed at **2,397 ms** and a later build measured **2,365 ms**, both against its **2,000 ms** limit under SwiftShader; all functional checks in that file passed in both runs. The historical same-environment software-rendering exception and baseline comparisons are in MAP_PERFORMANCE_RELEASE.md; these are not performance passes. The final imported `polygon` layer-kind and layer metadata change passed the local selection regression. Production deployment must be checked against the final pushed commit.
